Analytics notice

For visitors outside the EU, EEA and Quebec, on public pages (landing, pricing, tour, sign-up, sign-in), Google Analytics loads on arrival and this notice says so. It replaces the consent banner for them. It never blocks the page or the tour.

  • OK hides it for good. It saves that the notice was seen, not consent, so app pages still ask.
  • Cookie settings opens today's settings panel, unchanged. Saving there is a real choice and wins everywhere.
  • Desktop: bottom-left, clear of the tour panel and its button, which sit bottom-right. Phone: top, because the tour panel takes the bottom of the screen.
  • It sits under the tour's intro dialog, so a click on it can't close the dialog by accident. It's dimmed until the dialog closes.
  • Visitors in the EU, EEA and Quebec, visitors whose country can't be read, and all app pages keep today's banner.
