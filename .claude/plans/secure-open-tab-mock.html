Secure open tab

The tab that opens when someone opens a document from Firma, while Firma checks or grants access and before it hands over to OneDrive or Google Drive. Today it's about:blank with one gray line. Each frame below shows the tab title and address bar too. The first frame plays the real sequence; the rest hold one state each.

Live sequence

Waiting, then the slow line at 6s, then the Done check and the hand-off.

1 · Waiting (0 to 6s)

The workspace loader's mark and motion. The file, where it lives, and what is happening.

2 · Slow (after 6s)

Same layout, the line changes. A OneDrive grant has been measured at 16s.

3 · Access ready, handing off

The mark turns into the Done check, as the workspace loader does on arrival, then the tab moves to the document. Shown for about 400ms; not a pause added for show.

1 · Waiting, Google Drive

The same page for a Google Drive file. If the caller doesn't know the provider, the line drops the name: "Checking your access, then opening it".

4 · Couldn't open

Says why, in the route's own words, and gives a way back. Color only on the alert tile.

5 · Check your email (Google, optional)

Today the tab just closes when Google sends its sharing email. It could say so instead. Your call.

Notes

  • "Where it lives" is the storage name only (OneDrive, SharePoint, Google Drive). The hook doesn't know the provider until the grant answers, so callers would pass it in (the Files list already knows isOneDriveClient); when it's unknown the line leaves the name out.
  • Firma's favicon in the tab, and the lockup top-left, both neutral. The mark and its colors come from the workspace loader unchanged: blue Underway ring while waiting, green Done check on hand-off, red only on the alert tile.
  • "Back to Firma" closes this tab (allowed, since Firma opened it) and lands on the page it came from, where the dialog shows the same reason. "Try again" asks for access again from this tab.
  • No em dashes; a middle dot between label parts. The role="status" region announces the line changes.
