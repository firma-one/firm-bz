  • Automatic retries: every upload (files, folders, duplicates) tries twice more, after 1.5 seconds and then 4 seconds, when the connection drops, it times out, or it gets a 5xx or 429. Folder uploads used to make no retries at all.
  • No retry on a refusal like a 403, since trying again can't change the answer.
  • Creating a subfolder during a folder upload retries the same way. If it still fails, a toast names the folder, so the upload no longer stalls on "Preparing upload…" forever.
  • A finished run with no failures still clears itself after 8 seconds, as before.
