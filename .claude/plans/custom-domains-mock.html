Note to the Group admin · required to reject
Approve unlocks once the check passes
  • Who sees what: the section appears only to Group admins (the people who manage billing). Members and clients never see it.
  • Emails: a System Admin gets one when a request arrives; the Group admin gets one when it's approved, not approved, or becomes unreachable.
  • "Add for a group": opens the same form with a workspace picker and skips approval. That's how datasentry, keithmeyer and scarbluu get assigned after deploy. app.firma.bz stays neutral.
  • Sign-in hand-off has no screen of its own: it's a server redirect of under a second. If it fails, people simply stay on www.firma.bz, signed in.
  • Not in this round: adding the Vercel domain through the API, and a branded address showing only its own workspace.
